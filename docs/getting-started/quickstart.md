# Quickstart & First Switch

Once installed, RigSwitch lives quietly in your Windows notification area (System Tray).

---

## 1. System Tray Controls

Look for the dynamic RigSwitch icon in your taskbar notification area:
* **Emerald Ultrawide Monitor** (`#00C853`): Indicates your **Desk Setup** is active.
* **Molten Orange F1/GT3 Yoke** (`#FF5500`): Indicates your **Sim Rig Setup** is active.

### Tray Actions:
* **Single Left-Click**: Flips instantly between Desk and Sim Rig modes.
* **Right-Click Context Menu**:
  * 🖥️ **Switch to Desk Setup**: Expands into presets submenu (*Work*, *Media*, *Clean Desk*).
  * 🏎️ **Switch to Sim Rig Setup**: Expands into presets submenu (*GT3 / Circuit*, *Rally / Drift*, *Flight / Space*).
  * ⚙️ **Settings...**: Opens the OLED Settings Window.
  * ❌ **Exit RigSwitch**: Gracefully unregisters hotkeys and terminates background services.

---

## 2. Default Global Hotkeys

You can toggle profiles or trigger direct presets from any application or full-screen game:

| Shortcut | Target Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> | **Toggle Profile** (Desk ⟷ Sim Rig) |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>D</kbd> | **Desk Setup** (Activates active Desk preset) |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>R</kbd> | **Sim Rig Setup** (Activates active Rig preset) |

---

## 3. Configuring Your Hardware

1. Right-click the tray icon and select **Settings...** (or launch `RigSwitch.App.exe` directly).
2. Under the **Workstation Profiles** tab:
   * Select your **Desk Display** and **Primary Audio Endpoint**.
   * Optionally select a **Fallback Audio Endpoint** (e.g. monitor speakers if your DAC or headphones are disconnected).
   * Select your **Sim Rig Display** and **Cockpit Audio Endpoint**.
3. Click **Save Settings**.
4. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> to test your switch!
